## 1. Platform Overview

**NUVRA** is a unified football management and discovery platform. It has transitioned to a **Unified Community Model** designed to bring together organizers and players in a flat, accessible ecosystem.

Instead of rigid club structures, NUVRA focuses on:
- **Game Discovery:** Easily find and join pickup games.
- **Social Performance:** Track your stats across different games and venues.
- **Easy Management:** Organizers can set up games, manage slots, and handle payments in one place.

---

## 2. User Roles at a Glance

All users share a unified identity system, but can act in different capacities:

| Role | Key Abilities |
|---|---|
| **Organizer** (Club Owner) | Create games, set pricing, upload payment QR codes, and approve/reject player bookings. |
| **Player** | Browse games, join teams, upload payment receipts, and track individual performance stats. |

## 3. Getting Started

### 3.1 Registration & Login

NUVRA uses a single, unified authentication system. You no longer need separate accounts for different portals.

1. **Sign Up:** Create an account using your email and a password.
2. **Onboarding:** After registering, you can set up your initial profile, including your playing position and basic info.
3. **Login:** Use your credentials to access the platform.

### 3.2 Google Login

You can skip the manual registration by clicking **Continue with Google**. This will automatically create your NUVRA account using your Google profile information.

## 4. For Players: Finding & Joining Games

The core of the NUVRA experience is the **Community Feed**.

### 4.1 Browsing the Game Feed

Navigate to the **Feed** to see all upcoming games. Each game card displays:
- **Title & Venue**
- **Date & Time**
- **Available Slots:** Real-time count of confirmed spots remaining.
- **Price:** The fee to join the game.

### 4.2 Joining a Game

1. Click on a game to view the **Game Details** page.
2. Review the game description and current roster.
3. Click **Join Game**. 
4. Select your team (**Team A** or **Team B**).
5. Your status will initially be set to **Pending**.

### 4.3 Payment & Receipt Upload

To confirm your spot, you must complete the payment:
1. Locate the **Payment QR Code** on the Game Details page.
2. Scan the code with your banking or e-wallet app and complete the transfer.
3. Take a screenshot of your **Payment Receipt**.
4. Click **Upload Receipt** on the Game Details page and select your screenshot.
5. Your status will change to **Awaiting Approval**. Once the organizer confirms your payment, your status becomes **Confirmed** and you are officially in the game!

### 4.4 Withdrawing from a Game

If you can no longer make it, you can click **Leave Game** on the Game Details page. Note that refund policies are managed by the individual game organizer.

## 5. Player Dashboard & Profile

### 5.1 Performance Analytics

Your personal dashboard shows your progress across all games you've played:
- **Match Stats:** Goals, assists, and minutes played.
- **Performance Rating:** A visual chart showing your recent form based on organizer ratings.
- **Attribute Wheel:** A breakdown of your skills (Pace, Shooting, Passing, Dribbling, Defending, Physical).

### 5.2 Profile Customization

You can personalize your presence in the community:
- **Avatar:** Upload a profile picture so others can recognize you.
- **Profile Info:** Update your preferred position, height, weight, and strong foot.

### 5.3 Member Directory

Use the **Members** section to find other players, view their public profiles, and see their stats. It’s a great way to discover talent and connect with the community.

## 6. For Organizers: Managing Games

If you are a **Club Owner** or organizer, you have access to specialized management tools.

### 6.1 Creating a Game

1. Navigate to **Create Game** in your dashboard.
2. Fill in the logistics: Title, Venue, Date, Time, and Team Names.
3. Set the **Total Slots** and the **Price** per player.
4. **Important:** Upload your **Payment QR Code** so players know where to send their fees.
5. Click **Create Game** to make it public.

### 6.2 Booking Approval Flow

As players join and upload receipts, you must manage their bookings:
1. Go to the **Game Details** page for your game.
2. View the **Bookings List**. Players with uploaded receipts will appear at the top as **Awaiting Approval**.
3. Review the uploaded receipt image.
4. Click **Approve** to confirm their spot (this decrements the available slots) or **Reject** if the receipt is invalid.

### 6.3 Cancelling a Game

If a game cannot proceed, you can use the **Cancel Game** option. This will notify all joined players and mark the game as cancelled in the feed.

### 6.4 Community Announcements

Organizers can post **Announcements** to the community feed. Use this for general updates, rules, or news that isn't specific to a single game.

### 6.5 Organizer Analytics

Access the **Analytics** dashboard to see the health of your community:
- **Total Revenue:** Summary of collected fees.
- **Player Growth:** Tracking new members.
- **Game Activity:** Stats on how many games are being filled.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Do I need a separate account for "Club" and "Community"?**  
A: No. NUVRA is now a unified platform. One account gives you access to everything.

**Q: Why is my status "Awaiting Approval" after I uploaded my receipt?**  
A: The game organizer must manually verify your receipt. Please allow some time for them to review it. Once approved, you'll see your status change to "Confirmed".

**Q: How do I become an organizer?**  
A: Users with the `club_owner` role can create games. If you wish to organize games, please contact the platform administrator to have your role updated.

**Q: Can I change my team after joining?**  
A: To change teams, you must first leave the game and then re-join selecting the other team (if slots are available).

**Q: My stats haven't updated after a game. What's wrong?**  
A: Stats are recorded by the organizer after the game is completed. If your stats are missing, reach out to the game organizer.
